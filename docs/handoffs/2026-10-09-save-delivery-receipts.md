## Change and scope

New saves previously counted Android service starts as accepted work and fetched covers before the service could refuse the audio request. One prepared batch now keeps its producer reservation until every service result or a 60-second uptime deadline. At most 128 pending receipts and 4MiB logical preserved request/cover text/overhead, whole-batch preflight. Single deadline runnable, cancelled on completion; no permanent retired-token map. Exact shelf identity, full request equality, normal stop reason and single-use process-local token are checked before command budgets or move ownership. A process-restart/unknown, expired, malformed or replayed intent becomes INIT, not a mutation.

The actual phone/card service wraps Media3's onStartCommand: only a successfully forwarded, admitted Add counts as queued. A queued request is not proof of persisted/completed audio. Cover work is optional and starts only after that acknowledgement. Manager limits/move admission, card binding failure, null startService return and send/service exceptions are refusals; feedback counts accepted/refused/unconfirmed requests separately. A timed-out selection must be checked in Saved copies before retrying missing songs. Malformed intent copies leave the legitimate receipt available until delivery or timeout.

Existing move tokens/source-removal authority, untagged legacy service policy, originals, index/schema, packages, signing and release behavior remain. This is the new-save producer path only: bulk removal delivery, full native cache/played/destructive holders and final/UI queue limits still require engineering. The app's pending holder and submitted batch are bounded; Android's already-submitted Binder/start backlog after timeouts is not a proved total platform-heap bound. Deadline uses uptime: sleep can postpone expiry. Neither preflight nor acknowledgement proves the manager will download successfully.
