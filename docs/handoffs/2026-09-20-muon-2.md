## Preserve these implementation decisions and limits

- **#60 overlay:** three tabs; saved player/lyrics intent; Back closes Lyrics, then player, then returns to Library. Controller detachment during Activity stop must not clear player intent. A connected empty player may close it; pause/ended with a current item does not. Hidden tabs are excluded from accessibility. Whole-player drag and predictive Back remain unfinished under #43.
- **#63 swipe:** deliberate horizontal artwork drag, quarter-width threshold with 48dp minimum, unavailable-direction resistance and spring back. Commit revalidates controller/current media ID/index/queue size, preventing ordinary automatic A→B advancement during a held drag from triggering B→C at release. It does not yet preview adjacent art. Same-length queue replacement needs stronger identity when editing is added; see [#47 note](https://github.com/averylicious/muon/issues/47#issuecomment-5750533820).
- **#61 discovery:** typed scan states, queued resolves, generation/request guards and logical timeouts. Legacy Android OS resolve work can outlive the logical timeout; do not claim OS cancellation. Auto-connect, durable moved-server identity and redesigned Connect UI remain unfinished under #39/#51. Old callback compatibility cannot retract lost rows; new UI should use snapshots.
- **#62 metadata:** album artist and positive duration are informational MediaMetadata. Seeking still uses actual Player.duration. No queue screen/actions, ordering, entry identity or Undo was implemented.
- **#58 grouping:** preserve original ordering, group by the documented album/artist rules, and do not mark ordinary List-backed models Immutable without justification. Read the contract before exposing groups in Albums/Artists UI.
- **#23 performance:** measurement-first; no current phone-profiling authorization. Ordinary track rows request Tauon's small thumbnails, so do not repeat the obsolete assumption that every row decodes 1000px artwork. Medium/full-player artwork is a separate path. Keep the thumbnail assessment/dependency before Albums; agree how to resolve it without speculative patches.
- **#47 queue:** agree shuffle order, Play next, current-entry removal, duplicate entry identity and Undo behavior with the user/Claude before backend implementation; preserve any explicitly approved decisions. Do not infer final semantics from a mockup.
- Tauon remains trusted-LAN-only. #26 backend correctness/reliability, #53 partial library loading, #16 adaptive layout and the remaining UI issues are still separate work, not silently completed by the green builds.
