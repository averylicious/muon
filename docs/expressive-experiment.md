# Material 3 Expressive experiment (Canary only)

This branch, `claude/m3-expressive-alpha`, tries Material 3 Expressive on Muon Canary. It never merges to `main`, and Stable is untouched. Test builds come from the branch's own **Android APKs** runs: the `app-debug-<commit>` artifact updates Muon Canary in place.

## What changed in the build

- **Material 3 1.5.0-beta02** (from alpha29 on 2026-10-23), named directly, because the newest Compose BOM (2026.09.00) still maps material3 to 1.4.0 stable. The alphas pulled in the Compose 1.13 alphas; beta02 needs only Compose 1.12.0, so the rest of Compose is the BOM's stable 1.12.1. Its AAR still requires compileSdk 37 and AGP 9.1 or newer, which the build already meets.
- **AGP 9.4.1 and Gradle 9.6.0.** The alpha's AAR metadata requires AGP 9.1 or newer and compileSdk 37. AGP 9 compiles Kotlin itself, so the modules no longer apply `org.jetbrains.kotlin.android`; the root still declares it (not applied) to pin the Kotlin Gradle Plugin at the project's version.
- AGP 9 defaults that had to be restored: `resValues` (the launcher labels) is enabled in `app`, and `android.onlyEnableUnitTestForTheTestedBuildType=false` keeps CI's release unit tests.

## What to try on the phone

**Settings → Material 3 Expressive experiment** has one switch per part, so each can be compared with it off on the same phone.

- **Expressive motion** covers the motion and every Expressive component:
  - Material's own controls use `MotionScheme.expressive()`. Muon's transitions use Expressive's springs instead of eased tweens: what moves or resizes (overlays, pages, the rail, the mini player, list reordering) uses the default spatial spring, which overshoots a little; what fades or changes colour uses the effects springs, which don't. Full-screen overlays (Lyrics, Queue) use the spatial stiffness without the overshoot, so their far edge never lifts off the screen. The player sheet's own settle spring is unchanged.
  - The mini player becomes Now Playing and turns back into it: Material's container transform (`PlayerMorph.kt`). There is one panel, not a second one fading in over the mini player. It starts as the mini player's own rectangle, in its colour and showing its content, and grows to the whole screen. As it grows, its colour turns into Now Playing's, the mini player's content fades out and Now Playing's fades in, and the cover flies from the thumbnail to its place. The dimming behind it deepens with the growth, so a drag that has barely begun, or has come back down, dims nothing. Opening, closing and both drags morph, and the finger still moves the panel one to one. Sideways, where the player is a side panel, it slides in as before.
  - Songs, Albums, Artists and Playlists are a connected button group (`ToggleButton`s), as is the cache limit in Settings, replacing chips and segmented buttons. The navigation bar is `ShortNavigationBar`, Expressive's flexible bar, and sideways the rail is `WideNavigationRail` (collapsed). The sort menu is Expressive's grouped menu (`DropdownMenuPopup`, `DropdownMenuGroup`, `SelectableDropdownMenuItem`), its chosen order on the app's secondary container rather than Material's default tertiary. Two parts stay Muon's own on purpose: the scroll indicator, because Material's `nonInteractiveScrollbar` is visual only and ours drives the draggable A–Z scroller; and the player's drag handle, which already matches Material's (32 × 4 dp, rounded, on-surface-variant) without the empty screen-reader node Material's adds.
  - Now Playing's Previous, Play and Next are a `ButtonGroup`: the pressed button widens and squeezes its neighbours. Shuffle and Repeat are tonal toggle buttons that turn from round to a rounded square when on.
  - The play button, in Now Playing, the mini player and the side panel, morphs between `MaterialShapes`: a nine-sided cookie while paused, a rounded square while playing, turning a little as it goes.
  - Icon buttons, Play and Shuffle on albums, artists and playlists squash while pressed.
  - On this device follows the Refined direction the user chose on 2026-10-23 (mockup: Muon Expressive Redesign). Each section's rows join into one group with round ends and tight joins, and the playing copy becomes its own rounded pill on the secondary container. Each row's ⋮ sits at the far right, centred however many lines the row has, and opens the same panel as a long press: the details sheet, which now also holds Update storage format once the fresh check for that copy confirms it. A storage card replaces the info line and the listening paragraph: downloads stay until deleted, and the played-song cache's fill shows as a still wavy meter against its limit, with "These play only from this phone. Why?" opening the same explanation. Rows fade in and out and the rest move into place on Show/Hide and Delete; the chevron turns and the row corners and colour animate.
  - On this device's "Preparing playback… Cancel" floats as a pill above the bars rather than as a row at the top of the list, which pushed every copy down and back up on each tap (the user's recording, .1370). It waits 300 ms before showing.
  - Predictive Back on Library pages (issue #658), following Android's predictive back design guidance for a detached surface: while Back is held on an album, artist or playlist, the page shrinks to 90%, its corners round and it shifts `(width / 20) − 8` dp with the finger (decelerated), while the page Back leads to shows underneath under a light scrim. Letting go fades the card away; cancelling springs it back. It seeks the page `AnimatedContent` (`SeekableTransitionState`) from a `PredictiveBackHandler`, and shared covers are off during the preview so they don't come apart from the card; the Back button keeps the flying cover. Pages opened from Search, which Back returns to the Search tab, keep plain Back.
  - Queue and Lyrics now cover Now Playing instead of putting the player away, and a held Back gesture shrinks them to a card over it. Once either has fully covered the player, the player leaves composition, so it costs nothing while you read; it comes back for a Back preview or when the overlay closes, which now reveals Now Playing in place rather than growing it again.
  - On this device, opened from Settings, now steps in and out like a Library page and has the same predictive Back, with Settings underneath.
  - Song lists (Songs, playlists, an artist's songs and Search results) are joined groups like On this device: rows on a tonal ground, 2 dp apart (Material's segmented gap), with round ends and tight joins, and the playing song as its own pill. They use Material's `SegmentedListItem` with On this device's corners (22/6 dp) rather than its 16/4 dp defaults, so both pages match; a pressed row still morphs. A swipe to queue uncovers its action in the row's own shape. 
  - The rest of the Refined mockup: album tracks (numbered) and the Queue's Next up are joined groups too, with the playing row as the same 22 dp pill; a dragged Next up row casts its own shadow inside the group's inset, and a swipe uncovers Remove in the row's shape. Settings' played-song cache row shows On this device's still wavy meter, and the Library's order control is a tonal pill.
  - A second pass against the Refined mockup (the user's review, 2026-10-24): Settings rows lead with icons (eight new ones in the generated set), Connected has a status dot and On this device a chevron; On this device has a large title with a summary, counts and sizes in its card, and each copy's size; the song panel leads with Play, Play next and Add to queue as one connected group (Play starts the list it was opened from), then a joined group naming the album and artist; Queue rows lead with their handle and end in a ⋮ with Remove; an album page centres a large cover over its title; Now Playing has a ⋮ for the playing song's panel; Search shows matched artists as avatars. Also: the playing song's row says Playing or Paused under its credits; Jump back in names the album on its large covers, fading as a cover narrows; the album order sits under Jump back in; a pressed button in a connected group widens as its neighbours give way; and On this device's counts roll to their new value. Left as they were, on purpose: the flat seek bar (#40), the volume slider, the play button's cookie-to-square morph, and no album ⋮ (there is no album panel yet).
  - Search keeps recent searches on this phone (`RecentSearches.kt`): a search is remembered when it is submitted or one of its results is opened, newest first, at most eight, and offered as chips at the top of Search; Clear forgets them. Muon's preferences are never backed up. Settings rows centre their icon and control however many lines they have, and a disabled row keeps its ground.
  - Now Playing's Lyrics button is a tonal pill when Tauon has lyrics for the song (`hasLyrics` from the library, since the player's item doesn't carry it), and plain text otherwise, so it is clear before opening them; synced or not. TalkBack hears "Lyrics available".
  - On album, artist and playlist pages, Play, Shuffle and Save copies are one connected group: Play (filled, widest), Shuffle and an icon-only Save copies (labelled for TalkBack), round at the outer ends and tight between, each fully round while pressed. The row below now only says what Save copies keeps ("Save copies keeps 12 songs, about 48 MB on this phone."). With Expressive motion off, the two rows are as before.
  - The song panel (long press or ⋮) leads with Play next and Add to queue as one connected pair, round at the outer ends and tight between, each fully round while pressed; Save a copy, Go to album and Go to artist stay a list below. Play isn't added: a row tap already plays within its list, and from the panel it would be unclear what it plays.
  - Every song row with a song panel has a ⋮ at its far right that opens the same panel as a long press (`SongMenuButton`): Songs, playlists, artist pages, Search and album pages, at the user's request on 2026-10-23. The Queue keeps its own swipe-to-remove and Remove control and has no song panel, so it has no ⋮.
  - A missing cover shows the cookie placeholder everywhere (lists, mini player, Now Playing, grid tiles, On this device): the paused play button's nine-sided cookie (`MaterialShapes.Cookie9Sided`) holding a drawn note, in the theme's own colours (the wallpaper's with Material You), chosen by the user on 2026-10-23 from the four candidates in the missing-art mockup. It replaces the text ♪ and the grooved record. It isn't behind the switch: it is a picture, not motion.
  - Haptics: Shuffle and Repeat give the system's toggle-on and toggle-off feedback as they morph, and dragging the A–Z scroller ticks lightly at each new letter (the tick is not behind the switch).
  - The rows of albums on an artist's page and in search results are carousels: near either edge each cover slides a little slower than the row inside its rounded frame, Material's `carouselParallaxScrollEffect` (the multi-aspect carousel's look on an ordinary `LazyRow`). Only the cover moves; the titles stay still.
  - On an album's or an artist's page, once Play and Shuffle scroll off the top, Expressive's `HorizontalFloatingToolbar` rises above the bars with them again: Play as its vibrant button, beside Back, Back to top and Shuffle. It sinks away when the header's buttons come back, and the list leaves room at its end so the last song clears it.
  - The Library greeting follows the time of day ("Good morning.", "Good afternoon.", "Good evening.", "Late-night listening."), and offline says "Your music, with you."; a new one rises into place over the old as the hour turns. This one is not behind the switch: it is copy, and the change animates with Muon's motion springs.
  - An album tile opened while partly hidden under the sort bar is first brought fully into view, so its cover flies from, and back to, the whole tile rather than over the view buttons.
  - Albums opens with **Jump back in** once three or more albums have been played: Material 3's `HorizontalMultiBrowseCarousel` of the albums played lately, most recent first, each cover growing as it scrolls into focus. Muon remembers the last ten, per server, as each song starts (`RecentAlbums.kt`).
  - Song rows are Material 3 Expressive list items (`ListItem` with `onClick`/`onLongClick` and shapes): the same cover, lines, length and download mark, and the current song on its tonal surface, but the row's corners morph while pressed. They use the plain click overload, so a song is not announced as a radio button.
  - A song row can be swiped: towards the end for Play next, towards the start for Add to queue. The row slides over the action, which lights up with a tick once the swipe has gone far enough; letting go there queues the song with the usual Undo, and the row springs back (`SwipeToQueue.kt`). The gesture is read before the row sees the touch (the Initial pass), so once it is a sideways swipe the row's tap is cancelled; a `draggable` version moved the row but never queued on release, and a quick swipe played the song instead (the Pixel, .348). Material's `SwipeToDismissBox` was not used: its veto callback was deprecated in 1.5.0-alpha29, and without it a row slides fully away before it can come back. TalkBack reaches both through the long-press song actions.
  - Settings rows are `SegmentedListItem`s, whose corners morph while pressed; Settings and Queue use `LargeFlexibleTopAppBar`, and Settings' subtitle names the installed build.
  - Pull to refresh and the Connect scan show the shape-morphing `LoadingIndicator`; connecting and downloads show wavy progress bars. While a track buffers, the seek bar itself turns into a wavy bar in its own place (a plain one with Expressive motion off), so nothing around it moves; it waits 300 ms first, so the brief stall at each skip does not flicker it. The seek bar was flat, as decided on #40 under material3 1.4.0; on 2026-10-24 the user asked to try the wave again on beta02, so with Expressive motion the played part of the track is a wave that rolls while the song plays and smooths out flat when it pauses, buffers or the thumb is held (`WavySeek.kt`). It is drawn in the stock `Slider`'s track slot, so dragging, taps and TalkBack's seek actions are unchanged. The landscape player panel's progress bar, which can't be dragged, waves the same way with Material's `LinearWavyProgressIndicator`.
- **Blur** covers two things:
  - Now Playing sits on its song's cover, blurred and stretched behind the whole player under a veil of the cover-tinted background, so each song glows in its own colours. It is blurred once per song on a 40 px copy, so it costs nothing per frame and works on every Android version. Pure black keeps its black player.
  - The library blurs behind the player as it rises, eased in: the blur follows the square of how far the player has risen, so a short pull or the last moment of closing barely blurs, and the full blur arrives as the player settles. It also blurs behind the song actions sheet. This part needs Android 12 or newer.
  - The mini player and the navigation bar are frosted glass: the lists scroll on underneath them, and the covers show through blurred, under the bars' own colours at 65% with a 32 dp blur, nearer Android 17's own (`FrostedGlass.kt`). The blurred copy starts with the page colour and is clipped to the bars, so neither the sharp list nor a blur fringe shows. The flexible navigation bar gets 6 dp more room above its pill, which otherwise looked cut short against the edgeless glass. Compose has no backdrop blur, so the tab content records itself into a layer, and the bars draw that layer, moved to where they sit and blurred, behind themselves. Each list adds the bars' height to its own end padding so its last row still scrolls clear, and the scroll indicator and A–Z scroller stop above the bars. Limits: Android 12 or newer, portrait only (sideways the rail and side panel stay solid), and one GPU blur pass per frame while the bars are shown. The mini player wears its song's cover colours, as Now Playing does (`ArtworkTheme`), so its tint over the glass, its play button and the panel the player grows from all carry the cover's hue; the navigation bar keeps the library's. The player's panel starts as that same glass: the same blurred library under the same translucent tint (`frostedPanel`, its own layer), the tint thickening to Now Playing's colour as the panel grows, so a short pull fades the blur out rather than switching it off in one frame. The song menu is not frosted.

## Known limits

- The library blur is Compose's `RenderEffect` on the library's own layer, not the system's cross-window blur, so it matches Android 17's look rather than reproducing SystemUI's tuning. It costs a GPU pass per frame while the player is up; watch for dropped frames on the Poco.
- Alpha APIs can change or vanish before 1.5.0 is stable. Anything adopted from here needs re-checking against the stable artifact.
- With Expressive motion on, a Settings switch row is announced as a switch, but the rows no longer carry the custom click labels ("Refresh library", "Disconnect") that the hand-built rows had.
