# Cooperative cancellation during library admission (#253)

LibraryModel moves aggregate label/metadata/artist-credit admission to IO, but those synchronous loops did not check their coroutine Job. Disconnect/load cancellation revoked publication, yet a large old batch could continue encoding/counting records/expanded credits before withContext returned, overlapping a new load. This is source-confirmed avoidable work/lifetime, not a phone OOM, leak or measured latency claim. Parser internals and a single encoding/token remain separate, finite work; this is cooperative cancellation at admission boundaries.

Constructor label admission, fresh rows, semicolon-credit traversal and retained-fallback admission now accept a synchronous cancellation checkpoint. Production supplies the CURRENT IO call's coroutine context for each constructor/add/finish. It never stores the constructor's short-lived child Job for later calls: that child is completed by the next add. Cancellation propagates as CancellationException; old visible library/publication ownership and policy limits stay unchanged. Batch counters/loaded map commit only after successful complete admission. Finish remains one-shot even on cancellation/failure.

One focused control cancels an actual Job as an AbstractList supplies its first record. Fresh and retained-fallback admission must stop without reading the second record, preserve old list identity, and leave a cancelled fresh batch's count/byte/credit budget available for a subsequent admitted batch. Already-cancelled constructor admission also refuses. Existing encoded-byte, duplicate/fallback/order/credit rollback controls remain. CI is first Android compile/run. Source self-check is not independent review; manual user cancellation/refresh interaction remains pending.
