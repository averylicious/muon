Pinned Media3 DownloadService.onCreate caches its selected helper/manager per concrete service class, and later service instances reuse it without calling Muon's selection again. Changing Store.card or destroying/restarting the service therefore does not establish a changed destination. An initial absent-card service can retain the phone manager after insertion; an originally selected card manager can remain selected after replacing the Store.card field.

These tests characterize ownership only, not active-operation or wrong-destination data loss.

The fixture alone clears helper state while it owns all fixture services; it destroys services and releases mounted managers/caches before ending. A helper redirect must coordinate all active service/manager/callback owners and separately solve mapping preservation. Throwing from getDownloadManager or falling back to phone is not an established safe recovery design.
