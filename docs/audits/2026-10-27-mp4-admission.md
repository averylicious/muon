## Admission before the real parser

`PlaybackExtractors` forwards ordinary `Mp4Extractor` reads through a stateful input observer. The actual8-byte header is checked before Media3 allocates a parsed leaf. Extended sizes, parent/file ends, overflow, size0, the pinned small-free workaround and meta headers retain their relevant semantics. Parsed leaves share a32MiB retained-byte budget,4,096 parsed-box limit and depth16. Whole mdat/file size is not capped. Seek0 resets; nonzero source reopen keeps pending state.

The real leaf array is checked after its body read and before native parsing. Sample/offset/timing/edit/description counts need physical backing. At moov completion, all tracks share a weighted table-row budget, including primary/edit/accumulated tables and rechunk arrays. A large unparsed final child can trigger native moov completion without another read; admission therefore also runs at that child's header.

For one proven raw sample description with only known harmless framed children (chan/free/btrt), the guard uses a conservative PCM frame/rechunk bound, following the actual QuickTime/ordinary prefix. **Ambiguity is not assumed safer:** multiple descriptions, encryption or children can still select raw indexing independently of a small stsz count. Other fixed tracks are charged against both normal and worst-case chunk-based allocation paths. Unknown formats may be over-counted/refused. No byte-substring codec classification or hidden table-omission flag.

Format detection is separate: both plain and fragmented MP4 candidates use the pinned Sniffer. Its moov/uuid search can grow the peek buffer and its ftyp array is allocated before a payload peek. Peeks/advances are bounded before input-buffer growth; an ftyp over64KiB is refused at its header. These are resource policies, not assertions every oversized file is malformed.

## Nested metadata admission

A second step checks actual leaf bytes before BoxParser: keys and sample-description counts are backed and charged before their arrays; codec child boxes stay inside their sample entry; esds decoder-specific and IAMF LEB128 lengths fit before their byte arrays. Standard iTunes/mdta data/artwork, SMTA child framing and chapter counts are checked with a shared4,096-entry movie budget. Unknown opaque iTunes items are left uninterpreted, and unsupported QuickTime sound-description versions retain their skipped-entry behavior. No whole metadata copy or count-sized guard array.

This deliberately refuses malformed nested lengths that Media3 might previously drop. Codec bitstreams (for example SPS/AV1 internals), every exotic child format and downstream decoders are not certified. Fragmented reading/MFRA remains separate even though both sniffers are guarded.

## Proposed policy and compatibility

The user provisionally accepted the weighted-row limit on2026-10-27 after disclosure of the approximately2–3hour AAC impact; document it clearly. Other new limits/compatibility still need ordinary acceptance. Defaults:32MiB metadata/peek window,1,048,576 weighted table rows,16MiB declared sample cap,64KiB ftyp,4,096 parsed boxes,4,096 nested metadata entries and depth16. Roughly two row units per ordinary sample, or three with a single media edit, means long48kHz/1024-frame AAC can be refused around2–3hours. PCM with ambiguous descriptions may be refused earlier despite efficient actual rechunking. These costs are explicit; no long-audiobook or universal heap/decoder guarantee. Decoder overhead, nested allocations and other containers are not silently cleared.

The sample cap does not bound every downstream maxInputSize/decoder allocation. Pinned TrueHD grouping is considered for known entries; codec changes/encryption need further review. Unusual huge metadata padding may fail during sniff. A rejection is a permanent content/resource failure through existing player handling, not an infinite metadata retry.
