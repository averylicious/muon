## Likely defects

1. **Returning to the app blinks the player (confirmed by the user on their phone, 2026-11-10; also on main). Fixed on the experiment in [#767](https://github.com/averylicious/muon/pull/767), QA pending; the fix keeps the released controller published rather than the direction suggested below.** `MainActivity.kt:51` drops the controller in `onStop`; `PlaybackState.kt:53` then publishes an empty `PlaybackUi`. The first frame after `onStart` therefore has no current song until the new controller connects. In that gap the mini player starts its exit, an open Now Playing is presented closed and then reopened (`MuonApp.kt:278`, `:307`), and the playing row loses its highlight and "Playing" line, which changes its height. Fix direction: keep the last `PlaybackUi` while the controller is null; `ready` already disables the controls.
2. **Opening Now Playing can evict every list thumbnail (inferred).** The cover and both swipe neighbours are decoded at the cover's full size, up to the 1024 px bucket, about 4 MiB each (`NowPlayingScreen.kt:462-464`). The memory cache holds 12 MiB (`Artwork.kt:48`), and loads run two at a time (`Artwork.kt:46`), so lists may repaint covers slowly after the player closes. Fix direction: decode neighbours small until a swipe starts, or size the cache from the device's memory class.
3. **Pages opened from Search have no predictive Back preview.** `libraryBackPage` returns null for them (`PageBackPeek.kt:80`), which disables the handler at `MuonApp.kt:752`; Back jumps to Search with a plain fade. Back from the Search or Settings tab to Library has no preview either.
4. **Album pages lack swipe-to-queue.** Album tracks are hand-built rows (`AlbumScreens.kt:146`), not `TrackRow`, so the swipe that works in Songs, playlists, artists and search does nothing there, and the rows lack the pressed-corner morph.
5. **A quick flick on the big cover does not skip.** `swipeAction` (`ArtworkSwipe.kt:16`, called at `NowPlayingScreen.kt:408`) is distance-only; the mini player's `miniSwipeAction` (`ArtworkSwipe.kt:78`) accepts a flick.
6. **Scroll positions reset (inferred).** Settings (`SettingsScreen.kt:79`), the Search browse list and album pages keep their scroll inside content that `AnimatedContent` disposes. Leaving a tab, or opening On this device (third group down) and coming back, returns to the top; a held Back from On this device would preview Settings at the top.
7. **Two different exits from Queue and Lyrics.** Button/hardware Back fades and shrinks the card (`MuonApp.kt:1111-1127`); the in-app arrow slides it down.
8. **Lyrics reload on every open** with a "Loading lyrics…" flash (`LyricsScreen.kt:62`), and `TauonApi.kt:72` returns the text as stored, so LRC timestamps would show raw if any lyrics carry them (not checked against a real library).

## Quality-of-life candidates

- **Previous should restart the song first** (the user's addition, 2026-11-10; this review missed it). Done on the experiment in #767, QA pending.

- **Drag the whole player down to close.** Only the top bar drags today; the file's own comment defers the rest (`PlayerDismissDrag.kt:38-45`). The fixed portrait layout has no vertical scroll to conflict with.
- **Re-selecting the current tab** does nothing (`MuonApp.kt:631`); the usual behaviour is pop to the tab's root, then scroll to top.
- **Playback does not resume after the process dies.** Modes are persisted, but the queue and position are not, and the manifest has no media-button receiver, so a headset or Bluetooth Play cannot restart Muon.
- **Queue:** no "Clear upcoming"; no haptic on picking up or swapping a row.
- **Now Playing:** artist and album text are not tappable (only ⋮ → Go to); long titles ellipsize with no marquee; Lyrics does not keep the screen on.
- **Haptics are uneven:** present on swipe-to-queue, cover swipe and the alphabet scroller; absent on the mini-player skip swipe, the dismiss threshold and queue reorder.
- **No Light/Dark/System choice;** the theme follows the system only (`MainActivity.kt:29`).
- **The search query survives a disconnect** and shows against the next server's library (`MuonApp.kt:155`, reset list at `:716-725`).
- **Artists and Playlists are plain rows** while Songs are joined groups; their press ripple is an unclipped rectangle.
- **Swipe-to-queue ignores layout direction:** `SwipeToQueue.kt:55-58` uses physical right for Play next while its comment says "towards the end".

## Architecture suggestions

- **Extract a navigation state holder** from `MuonApp.kt`: one saveable class owning tab, open page, overlays and `back()`. The pure `backTarget`/`libraryBackPage` functions already exist and are tested; tab re-select and Back-from-Search fit there. No navigation library needed.
- **One predictive-Back helper.** The Library page, On this device and overlay handlers (`MuonApp.kt:686`, `:752`, `:1111`) repeat the same begin/collect/commit/restore sequence.
- **Route stray animation specs through `Motion.kt`:** the cover deal and spring (`NowPlayingScreen.kt:259-262`) and the press spring (`SharedMotion.kt:54`) ignore the Expressive switch.
- **`SeekControls` writes slider state during composition** (`NowPlayingScreen.kt:581`); move it to an effect.
- **`PlayerMorph.frame()`** walks layout coordinates from five or more draw-phase readers each frame of a drag; compute once per frame.
- **Three copies of the same "latest player" state** (`MuonApp.kt:75`, `:92`, `:162`).
